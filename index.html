Energy Efficiency

Reducing energy use is one of the simplest ways to lower our footprint.

You can help by making a few thoughtful choices throughout the day.

💡

Did you know?

Lighting can account for up to 15% of a building’s electricity use. Turning off lights in unoccupied spaces is a simple way to reduce energy consumption.

Waste Reduction

The most sustainable waste is the waste that is never created.

Explore the 5 R’s in order—prioritizing prevention before recycling.

Simple ways to help

  • Bring reusable bottles, mugs, and containers.
  • Print only when necessary.
  • Avoid single-use items.
  • Follow your office’s waste and recycling signage.
♻️

Did you know?

Reusable products often have a higher upfront environmental footprint than disposable alternatives, but that impact is spread over hundreds to thousands of uses, making them the more sustainable choice over time.
